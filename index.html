About This Project

The Psychological Assessment Tool is an educational project designed to help individuals aged 17–25 reflect on their preferences and behavioral tendencies. It explores 11 categories and presents a summary based on your responses.

How the Assessment Works

The assessment contains 55 questions divided into 11 categories, with five questions per category. Choose the answer that best reflects your usual preferences and behavior. There are no universally right or wrong answers.

How Scores Are Calculated

Each answer receives between 0 and 2 points according to the questionnaire's scoring rules. The points earned in each category are compared with the maximum possible points to produce a percentage score.

80–100% — High-scoring tendency: The category qualifies for an individual explanation.

60–79% — Moderate-scoring tendency: These categories qualify for individual explanations only when no category scores 80% or higher.

Below 60%: The score remains visible in your results, but the category does not receive an individual explanation.

Understanding Your Results

Your results highlight tendencies suggested by your answers. They are intended to support self-reflection, not to define your personality or determine your abilities. The categories and scoring ranges are part of this project's assessment design and should not be treated as clinically validated psychological measures.

Before You Begin

This assessment is intended for educational and self-reflection purposes only.

It is based on a study of psychological and behavioral tendencies observed within a selected group of individuals aged 17–25.

This assessment is not a medical examination, psychological diagnosis, or substitute for professional medical or psychological advice.

If you are experiencing psychological, emotional, or behavioral difficulties and require professional assistance, please consult a qualified psychologist, therapist, doctor, or other appropriate healthcare professional.
